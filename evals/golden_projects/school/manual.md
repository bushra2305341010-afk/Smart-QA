# School — User Manual

## Log in

Open the log-in page, enter your email and password, and select "Log in".
After logging in you see the dashboard with links for your role.
Select "Log out" to end your session.

## Students (administrators)

Open "Students" to see every student, sorted by name.
Select "Add student", fill in Name, Age, Student email and Parent email, and select "Save student".
A name must be 2 to 50 characters long and the age must be from 5 to 18.
To change a student, open the student and select "Edit student".
To remove a student, open the student and select "Delete student"; this cannot be undone.

## Classes and enrolment (administrators)

Open "Classes" and select a class to see its roster, capacity and average grade.
Choose a student in the "Student to enrol" list and select "Enrol student".
When the class is full you see the message "Class is full".

## Grades (teachers)

Open your class and select "Enter grades".
Type a whole number from 0 to 100 in the grade box next to the student and select "Save grade".
The letter grade and the class average update after each save.

## Attendance (teachers)

Open your class and select "Mark attendance".
Pick the date, choose Present, Absent or Late for each student and select "Save attendance".
You cannot mark attendance for a date in the future.

## Emailing parents

Open the student and select "Email parents" to send one message to the parent email.

## My grades (students)

Students open "My grades" to see their own grades, letter grades and attendance rate.

## Forgot your password

Open "Forgot password?" on the log-in page, enter your email, solve the CAPTCHA and select "Send reset link".
